Basic First Aid

Basic First Aid in Ontario equips individuals with the knowledge and practical skills needed to assist someone who has been injured or has become suddenly ill until emergency medical services arrive. Training emphasizes recognizing emergencies, conducting an initial assessment, activating the emergency response system, and providing appropriate care to help preserve life and reduce the risk of further harm.

Timeline — Blended Learning: Online: 3–5 hrs (self-paced) • In-person: 3.5 hrs
Fully In-Person: 7–8 hours

Formerly Standard First Aid

Intermediate First Aid

Intermediate First Aid is a more in-depth workplace first aid program designed to prepare participants to manage a wide range of medical emergencies and traumatic injuries until emergency medical services assume care. The course combines theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on skill development, emphasizing scene safety, casualty assessment, effective communication, and evidence-informed first aid interventions.

Participants learn to recognize and respond to conditions such as airway and breathing emergencies, circulatory problems, bleeding, shock, fractures, burns, environmental emergencies, and sudden medical illnesses. In Ontario, WSIB-approved Intermediate First Aid training meets provincial workplace first aid requirements.

Timeline — Blended Learning: Online: 7–8 hrs (self-paced) • In-person: 7–8 hrs
Fully In-Person: 16 hours

Good Questions

Choosing the right course

What is the difference between Basic and Intermediate First Aid in Ontario?

Basic First Aid (formerly Emergency First Aid) covers recognizing emergencies, calling for help, and providing care until EMS arrives — completable in as little as one day. Intermediate First Aid (formerly Standard First Aid) goes deeper into trauma, environmental emergencies, and sudden illness, and is what most Ontario employers need for WSIB compliance.

We offer the First Aid Program in English only — what about French?

We offer the First Aid Program in English only. To find a First Aid Program training provider that offers the full program in French, please visit the WSIB First Aid Program website.

Which CPR level do I need?

Level A covers adults only; Level C covers adults, children, and infants. Healthcare providers typically need CPR-BLS. If you're not sure which one applies, EMT Canada can help match you to the right level for your employer, school, or licensing body.

Should I choose Blended Learning or fully in-person?

Blended Learning is our most popular option — complete theory online at your own pace, then attend a shorter in-person session to practice and demonstrate your skills. Fully in-person covers both theory and practice in one sitting with a paramedic instructor.
